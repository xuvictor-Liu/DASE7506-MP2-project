# AI assistance and attribution

OpenAI Codex assisted with interpreting the project requirements, designing candidate features and experiments, writing training and inference code, running local evaluation, and drafting documentation. The student must review and understand the implementation and verify the final report before submission.

The supplied dataset, embeddings, parser, runner, scorer, and project guide originate from https://github.com/xudongwu-0/DASE7506-MP2. Their original attribution and license files are retained. No additional pretrained weights or external training examples are used.

Training reads only supplied training references. Development uses validation references. Test references are reserved for the unchanged data checker and final independent scorer. Predictions are produced from the bank and arrays supplied to the inference interface, without ID-based lookups.
